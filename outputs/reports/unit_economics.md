The estimate assumes one release and one completed client note per mandate-month. Model cost is computed from the prices in `params.yaml`; it is not a provider invoice.

The cost of failure is controlled by exact-payload approval and client isolation. The modeled incident-response labor floor is $960; reputational and contractual effects are deliberately left unpriced.
